# Sucralfate (Carafate / Ulcogant)

**Class:** Mucosal Protectant  
**Subclass:** — (lone contender)  
**Mechanism:** Topical barrier + cytoprotection (non-systemic)

---

## Overview

Sucralfate is a unique GI agent that is **not** an acid suppressor. It is a sucrose-aluminum complex that polymerizes at gastric pH <4 into a sticky gel that binds selectively to ulcerated mucosa (6–7× concentration vs normal tissue). It provides a physical barrier against acid/pepsin/bile back-diffusion while also stimulating local prostaglandin synthesis, mucus secretion, and bicarbonate production.

---

## Efficacy

| Indication | Healing Rate | Comparator |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| Duodenal ulcer (4 wk) | 51–79% | Cimetidine 71–77% (NS) |
| Duodenal ulcer (8 wk) | 76–86% | Cimetidine 92% (NS, −12% to +5%) |
| Gastric ulcer (8 wk) | 80–94% | Placebo 33–41% (p<0.005) |
| GERD (8 wk) | 31–60% | Placebo superior for symptom relief |
| Stress ulcer (ICU) | Similar to placebo/PPI | Lower pneumonia vs PPI/H2RA |

**Healing score:** 81% avg DU healing → 7/10 (between H2RA 5.5 and PPI 8-9)

## Pharmacokinetics

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Absorption | <3–5% systemic (acts intraluminally) |
| Onset | Immediate local binding (minutes) |
| Duration | ~6 h at ulcer site |
| Half-life | N/A (no meaningful systemic t½) |
| Dosing | 1 g QID (30 min ac + hs) or 2 g BID |
| Activation | Needs pH <4 to polymerize |

**PK score:** 4.0 — unique zero-absorption profile but QID dosing + acid requirement limit practicality.

## Safety

| Issue | Severity |
|-------|----------|
| Constipation | Mild (1–10%) |
| Drug interactions | Major (166: binds oral drugs) |
| Aluminum toxicity (CKD/ESRD) | Critical — avoid in ESRD |
| C. difficile risk | None |
| Acid rebound | None |
| Pregnancy | Category B (low risk, minimal absorption) |

**Safety score:** 8.5 — excellent for the right patient, but drug interaction burden + CKD contraindication prevent 10/10.

---

## Strengths

- Zero acid suppression — no rebound, no C. diff, no osteoporosis/B12 concerns
- Safest pregnancy option (Category B, minimal absorption)
- Bile salt adsorption — unique for bile reflux gastropathy
- Lower pneumonia risk vs PPI/H2RA in ICU
- Strengthens mucosal barrier (increases mucus thickness + viscosity)
- No systemic side effects

## Weaknesses

- QID dosing → poor adherence (~2× daily PPI)
- 166 drug interactions → significant management burden
- Slower healing vs PPI (4wk GU: 23% vs omeprazole 49%)
- Contraindicated in ESRD/CKD (aluminum accumulation)
- Not effective for moderate-severe GERD
- Requires acid pH → co-prescription with PPI is contradictory
- Smoking negates therapeutic benefit
- Higher relapse at 1yr (46% vs 36% omeprazole)

## Best For

1. **Pregnancy** — GERD/ulcer treatment with minimal fetal exposure
2. **Bile reflux gastropathy** — unique bile salt binding
3. **ICU stress ulcer prophylaxis** — when pneumonia risk minimization is priority
4. **Mild GERD in patients wanting no acid suppression**
5. **Mucosal protection without systemic effects**
